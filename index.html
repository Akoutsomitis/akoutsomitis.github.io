about me

hi, i'm Alex 👋

An artist and educator. I graduated from Pratt Institute with a BFA and a minor in Painting and Art History. I received my Masters in Teaching Special Education from LIU Brooklyn. My art practice is still in the works of being discovered.

intro to painting

6 weeks · all levels welcome

Start from zero. Colour mixing, brush handling, and how to look at what you're actually painting. You'll finish with a small body of work you're proud of.

ceramics.txt_▫✕

hand-built ceramics

4 weeks · no wheel needed

Pinch, coil, and slab building. We'll make dishes and vessels, then carve and glaze them. Everything gets fired between sessions.

  • clay
  • hands on
  • small group
adaptive-art.txt_▫✕

adaptive art

ongoing · ages 6–18

Art class built around how each student learns. Sensory-friendly materials, flexible pacing, and no wrong way to make something. Drawing on my special education training.

  • inclusive
  • kids & teens
  • 1:1 or group
